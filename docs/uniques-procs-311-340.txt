30 UNIQUES A DECLENCHEMENT — 311 A 340

15 procs de degats et 15 procs de buff. Propositions, pas effets deja implementes.
Leurs conditions, limites, origines, delais internes et budgets de base sont precises
dans regles-uniques-propositions.txt. Les nouveaux noms d'effet ne sont pas des
promesses de sorts deja presents dans ABILITIES.

Numero | Nom | Slot | Type | Description
311 | The Bonebreaker's Tally | Head | Damage proc | Every fifth successful offensive direct-skill activation releases a Physical nova around its primary target. A volley counts as one activation regardless of how many victims it hits. The nova deals its own damage and does not advance the counter.
312 | The Frost King's Shard | Ring | Damage proc | A direct skill critical strike has a 25% chance to send three guided Cold shards toward nearby enemies distinct from the primary victim. Without another target, no shards are launched. Two-second internal cooldown; shards cannot critically strike or trigger procs.
313 | The Funeral Ember | Feet | Damage proc | A victim killed by one of your skills or its native DoT has a 30% chance to leave a delayed Fire explosion at its position. 314 | The Knell of the Final Ticks | Neck | Damage proc | When one of your DoTs expires naturally on a living victim, it triggers a small Shadow explosion around that victim. Death, eviction, and DoT replacement do not count as natural expiration. Global four-second internal cooldown.
315 | The Palms of Caged Thunder | Hands | Damage proc | A successful block launches a Lightning discharge at the attacker, then at up to two nearby enemies. Each target is hit only once. If the attacker has disappeared, another target in range is chosen. Three-second internal cooldown.
316 | The Pauldrons of Returned Venom | Shoulders | Damage proc | Taking damage that actually removes health has a 25% chance to send a Nature projectile toward the attacker if still alive and in range. Blocking, immunity, and full absorption cannot trigger it. Three-second internal cooldown.
317 | The Sight of Five Names | Ranged | Damage proc | Hitting five different primary targets across your projectile activations releases a guided Arcane bolt at the toughest enemy in range. A volley counts only one primary target, and the counter clears on activation. The bolt does not advance the counter.
318 | The Buckle of Broken Shackles | Waist | Damage proc | Rooting an enemy that was not already rooted triggers a Physical explosion around it. Simply extending the control effect does not count. Bosses can trigger this effect if a reduced root is actually applied. Global three-second internal cooldown.
319 | The Choir of Three Schools | Trinket | Damage proc | Dealing damage with your skills from three different primary schools within eight seconds triggers a Holy explosion around the last target. Lightning and Nature count as one school for this counter. Marks are consumed; six-second internal cooldown.
320 | The Eye of the Hollow Center | Head | Damage proc | An area-skill activation that hits at least one enemy has a 15% chance to place an Arcane echo at its toughest target's position. The echo explodes one second later on enemies still present. One roll per activation; three-second internal cooldown.
321 | The Breastplate of Returned Scales | Chest | Damage proc | When an absorption shield expires naturally with protection remaining, it sheds three guided Physical scales toward distinct enemies in range. Their damage is shared from the remaining protection, subject to a global cap. A replaced or broken shield cannot trigger this effect; five-second internal cooldown.
322 | The Beacon of the Dark Step | Neck | Damage proc | Successfully activating your mobility skill on F triggers a Shadow nova after a short delay at the position you occupy then. This also works with a speed boost or vanish; it does not require a dash path. Ten-second internal cooldown.
323 | The Glove of the First Infection | Hands | Damage proc | A direct skill impact against a full-health enemy applies this item's own Nature infection for four seconds. This DoT is neither Deadly Poison nor a class disease and cannot trigger other procs. One infection per victim and owner; global two-second internal cooldown.
324 | The Boots of the Blazing Wake | Feet | Damage proc | Walking ten meters prepares a charge. Your next successful melee activation consumes it to create a Fire cone behind its primary victim. Teleports and forced movement do not fill the charge; only one charge can be stored.
325 | The Ring of the Ravenous Feast | Ring | Damage proc | Killing with a skill while below 35% health launches Shadow projectiles at up to three other nearby enemies. Proc kills cannot trigger it. Five-second internal cooldown; the projectiles cannot restore health or trigger another feast.
326 | The Diadem of the Foretold Strike | Head | Buff proc | Every fifth successful Attack activation prepares a six-second Omen. Your next offensive projectile activation consumes this buff and guarantees a critical strike on its primary target only. Omen does not stack or make periodic ticks critically strike.
327 | The Hide of Quenched Fevers | Chest | Buff proc | When one of your DoTs expires naturally, gain 25% damage reduction for four seconds. Death or replacement of the DoT cannot trigger it. Eight-second internal cooldown; another activation refreshes the buff without increasing its strength.
328 | The Rosary of Three Words | Neck | Buff proc | Casting three different offensive skills within six seconds grants 25% cooldown recovery to offensive skills for four seconds. Copies and procs do not advance the sequence. Eight-second internal cooldown; the sequence clears on activation.
329 | The Gauntlets of the Sweeping Riposte | Hands | Buff proc | Blocking prepares a Riposte for five seconds. Only one Riposte can wait.
330 | The Soles of Fleeting Refuge | Feet | Buff proc | Activating your mobility skill without an active absorption shield creates protection equal to 15% of your maximum health for three seconds. 331 | The Heart of the Crimson Threshold | Trinket | Buff proc | A hit that takes you from above 35% to below 35% health triggers temporary regeneration for three seconds. Staying below the threshold cannot trigger it again. Twenty-second internal cooldown; this proc regeneration cannot trigger other healing effects.
332 | The Belt of Two Disciplines | Waist | Buff proc | Successfully activating an Attack then a Spell, or the reverse, within four seconds grants 40% increased area for four seconds. 333 | The Mask of Patient Precision | Head | Buff proc | Three consecutive direct activations whose primary target does not receive a critical strike grant 50 additional points of critical multiplier for four seconds. 334 | The Bindings of Two Fevers | Wrists | Buff proc | Applying DoTs from two different schools to the same victim grants 25% damage over time for six seconds. Only DoTs applied during this window capture the bonus; existing instances are not strengthened retroactively. Eight-second internal cooldown.
335 | The Pauldrons of Unbroken Glass | Shoulders | Buff proc | A hit fully absorbed by a shield grants 15 percentage points of critical strike chance for five seconds. 336 | The Seal of the Fifth Guard | Ring | Buff proc | Every fifth successful activation of a shield, immunity, or thorns prepares a six-second Echo. 337 | The Veil of the Final Straw | Back | Buff proc | Losing more than 15% of your maximum health to one hit triggers a one-second vanish that makes ordinary enemies lose your trail. 338 | The Ring of the Suspended Harvest | Ring | Buff proc | After three seconds standing still, resuming normal walking grants an additional 200% XP pickup radius for four seconds. Teleporting does not trigger this buff. It does not stack or change meta XP or item quality.
339 | The Knot of Crossed Schools | Neck | Buff proc | Hitting the same victim with two different schools within three seconds prepares a six-second Relay.
